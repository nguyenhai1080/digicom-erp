# One bank receipt / multiple Invoice allocations — v0.6.115

## Owner scope and UI

Owner approved one bank transfer paying four Invoices with one fee and one evidence file. In **Quản lý Invoice → Invoice đã phát hành**, open **Nhận thanh toán nhiều Invoice**. Choose one customer, enter date/bank reference/grossUSD/feeUSD/actualUSD orVND/FX and optional PDF/PNG/JPG≤5MB once. Select2–50 current issued USD Invoices, across periods/pages; editable per-Invoice USD allocation supports partial payments. Server preview shows gross allocations, weighted fee/net-cash shares, remaining AR, shared evidence metadata; owner opens uploaded evidence and explicitly confirms. Every monetary/file/selection change invalidates preview/confirmation. The ordinary single-Invoice flow remains available.

Gross allocations must sum exactly to the transfer gross. NetUSD=gross−fee; netVND=round((gross−fee)×FX,0), HALF_UP. The example14430.23−10=14420.23USD; ×25500=367715865VND. Bank fee is entered, never assumed10USD or inferred from printed fee0. Fee and actual cash are independently apportioned for reporting by gross weights using integer largest remainders (cents/dong); deterministic InvoiceID ties, sums exact. A tiny share may be0. Allocated fee cents need not reconvert to the exact allocated VND at each row; the parent is the bank-money authority.
